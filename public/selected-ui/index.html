Questions shop owners ask

Do I need a website or a developer?

No. SeePay gives you a ready storefront at yourshop.seepay.shop. You add your products or services and share the link.

What does it cost?

A one-time setup fee of GH₵ 150. After that, each order has two fees: Paystack’s 1.95% processing fee and SeePay’s flat GH₵ 0.20. There is no monthly fee.

How do I get paid?

Customers pay with Mobile Money through Paystack. Each payment splits automatically. Paystack’s fee and SeePay’s GH₵ 0.20 come off, and the rest goes to the MoMo number you registered.

What kinds of business can use SeePay?

Any small business that sells products or services and takes payment by Mobile Money. Food, fashion, beauty, repairs, events, delivery and more.

Which Mobile Money networks work?

MTN, Telecel Cash and AT Money.

How do agents earn?

Share your referral code. When a shop you referred pays its setup fee, GH₵ 50 is added to your balance.
